The History of Manhattan Subway Kings.

MSK originally began in the fall of 1983. CHI, FURN, MADE, WASE were the first 4 members. We all attended JH52 from 1980-1984. Made msk is credited with the crew name and it originally stood for “Manhattan Subway Kids”. We were youngsters running the Dyckman (200 st) layups and battling crews from Brooklyn for dominance of the “A” line. Fame came quickly and expansion came heavy. We were the youngsters running with some heavy uptown gangster crews. “MR” betta is known as Manhattan Rebels, Renegades, and others. We all held down the blocks 200-207 St. Broadway to Post Ave. We all merged into a massive crew. “Manhattan Subway Kings” was born. Members included #Dia-president, #2-New-warlord, Magnum-vice Pres. #Chi, #Furn, Made, Wase, #Ram, #Dae, #Nie, #Sak, #Kc and the list goes on. We were a force to be reckoned in any yard north of 168. Including 175, 200, 225, bus yard, sanitation yard, and the ghost yard. After the Great Train Scrub late 80’s-90’s much of the crew moved on with their lives since there was no longer a “Subway Culture” Lots of the graffiti moved from the tunnels to the walls and then to the galleries, #MSK has never ended. The current crew heavyweights are #Kister #Frankism #Cone, #Kron, #Nare, #Dreb, #Cable, #Mile, #Kip #Cel,#Lets, #Richbudda #Aliven, #kerz, #zinc #Bester, #Devis2 #LP5#cen, #yayo, #Binho (brazil) #Bebar (paris), #Tore (Italy), #Gamo & #kitsa (Paris),#Maha (Australia) and many more still holding it down to date.
