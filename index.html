The History of Manhattan Subway Kings.

MSK originally began in the fall of 1983. CHI, FURN, MADE, WASE were the first 4 members. We all attended a preparatory school and had a common bond of becoming good friends who liked to hang out. We often hung out in the nice and rough neighborhoods of New York, and the city became a major part of our identity. We began as a small, local group before growing into something much bigger. This site preserves some of that history and the culture that shaped us.
